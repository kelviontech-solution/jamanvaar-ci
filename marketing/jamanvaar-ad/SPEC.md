# Production specification

50 seconds, 30fps. Nine scenes at 0, 5, 9, 15, 21, 27, 33, 39 and 43 seconds. Primary 1080x1920; landscape 1920x1080 uses its own layout rather than stretching the vertical.

Palette: cream #F7F4EC, teal #164E49, ink #102E33, muted gold #B6823C, white #FFFFFF. Typeface: local Segoe UI regular/semibold/bold. Headlines 82-98px portrait, 76-94px landscape; supporting copy 34-38px; image/demo provenance at least 28px. Title-safe 108px horizontal and 192px vertical on portrait. CTA held for seven seconds. No invented statistics or capability guarantees.

Photography: natural Indian hospitality, 35-50mm, warm daylight, realistic faces and hands. Subtle 1.00 to 1.035 image pushes. Screens remain front-facing and preserve aspect ratio. Product captures are labelled Local demo; generated scenes labelled Illustrative imagery. White/cream copy panels never cover the UI.

Motion: 18-frame power3.out entrances, 5-frame stagger, 10-frame outgoing wipe/slide; no endlessly drifting titles. Each scene reveals a single idea, then holds for reading. One grounded device/screen per product beat; brand reveal uses the real logo. No decorative KPI grids, fake chat, fake QR, particles, neon, excessive spinning or dark voids.

Shots: restaurant world 0-5; brand 5-9; POS 9-15; actual QR menu 15-21; illustrative WhatsApp 21-27; kiosk welcome/product preview 27-33; actual KDS ticket cards 33-39; actual QR owner console 39-43; logo/CTA 43-50.

QA: inspect every midpoint, first frame, both sides of every cut and the final readable frame; validate codecs/resolution/duration/audio peaks. Keep rendering logs and media provenance. Measure sound; do not claim listening if only signal analysis was performed.
