# Creative routes

The user supplied a nine-scene storyboard, so its restaurant-service storyline is selected.

1. **The owner's world (selected):** A warm restaurant moment opens into the actual product screens, then returns to the people they support. Photographic pushes, restrained screen reveals and a generous closing logo/CTA.
2. **One meal, many touchpoints:** A single fictional meal travels from menu to counter to kitchen. Not selected because the brief must introduce distinct products without implying a verified end-to-end integration.
3. **The restaurant, orchestrated:** Giant kinetic words and abstract shapes build an ecosystem map. Not selected because the requested authentic UI and hospitality photography make a more concrete introduction.
