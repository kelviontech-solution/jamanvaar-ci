# JAMANVAAR Admin Operations & Management Guide

## 1. Daily Operations Workflow

### Opening Shift:
1. Log into **JAMANVAAR Admin Control Plane** with authorized credentials.
2. Verify **Shift Opening Balance** and online status of all kiosk terminals.
3. Review **Menu Availability (86 list)**: Ensure dishes prepared for the day are marked active, and unavailable items are toggled to *Sold Out*.

### Shift Monitoring:
- **KDS Kitchen Board**: Monitor active KOTs, advance tickets through `PREPARING` and `READY`.
- **Table Occupancy**: Review live floor seating status.
- **Service Requests**: Respond promptly to customer calls for staff assistance from kiosks or tables.

### Shift Closing:
- Review **Today's Sales & Payment Method Breakdown** (UPI vs. Card vs. Cash).
- Verify all offline sync outbox events have completed.
- Export shift snapshot backup.
